Privacy Policy

Booth Fighting & Official Discord Administration Bot

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Booth Fighting and its associated Discord administration bot may process information when providing game moderation, administration, logging, and community services. The exact information processed depends on the features enabled and the system's actual configuration.

1. Information We May Process

Discord information

  • Discord user IDs, usernames, and display names.
  • Discord server and channel IDs.
  • Staff permissions and administrative access information.
  • Commands submitted to the administration bot.

Roblox information

  • Roblox user IDs and usernames.
  • In-game player information.
  • Player permission and administrative status.
  • Relevant game activity and moderation events.

Moderation records

  • Warnings and punishments.
  • Temporary and permanent bans.
  • Permission changes and removals.
  • Administrative actions performed through Discord.
  • Staff identifiers, action timestamps, and recorded reasons.
  • Relevant evidence associated with moderation incidents.

Game activity and messages

The system may record events occurring inside Booth Fighting for administration, security, troubleshooting, and moderation. Message or chat content should only be described as collected here if the game or bot actually records it. The exact information recorded depends on the logging features enabled.

2. Cross-Platform Processing

The Discord bot communicates with Booth Fighting's administrative systems to process authorized commands. For example, a staff member may issue a ban command through Discord, the system may identify the relevant Roblox player, and the game may process the action. The action may then be recorded in moderation logs. This can involve associating Discord staff identifiers with Roblox player identifiers.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to operate moderation systems, execute authorized commands, manage player permissions, investigate cheating and rule violations, maintain activity records, prevent unauthorized access, review staff activity, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain service security and reliability.

4. Data Storage

Information may be stored in databases, configuration files, server logs, or other infrastructure used to operate the services. The specific storage technology and hosting provider may change. Access to stored information should be restricted to authorized personnel and systems.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared with authorized Booth Fighting administrators and moderators, Discord and Roblox as needed to operate their services, hosting or infrastructure providers, and relevant parties when legally required or necessary to address security incidents.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Moderation records, activity logs, and administrative records may be retained for as long as necessary for security, moderation, operational, and legitimate record-keeping purposes. Users may contact the administration team to request access to or deletion of eligible information. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate security, moderation, or legal reasons.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored information and prevent unauthorized access. Administrative commands and staff permissions should be restricted to authorized personnel. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Platforms

Booth Fighting operates on Roblox, while its administration bot operates through Discord. Roblox and Discord have their own Terms of Service and Privacy Policies. Information processed by those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy practices. Booth Fighting is independently operated and is not automatically affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc.

9. Children's Privacy

The services must not knowingly collect or process personal information in violation of applicable age restrictions or privacy laws.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when new features, logging systems, or administrative functions are introduced. Updated versions will be published where this document is hosted.

11. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions, data access requests, eligible deletion requests, or concerns about moderation records, contact the Booth Fighting administration team through the official Discord support channels.
